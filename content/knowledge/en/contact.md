## Contact channels

You can reach me through whichever channel you prefer: LinkedIn, WhatsApp or e-mail. All three work well.

## Response times

On LinkedIn and WhatsApp I usually reply quickly, within minutes. I check e-mail during the day.

## Messages instead of calls

I prefer WhatsApp or written messages over a phone call. I work on-site and it's hard for me to take calls or video calls during the workday, so I'll reply faster by message.

## Salary and rates

I prefer to discuss salary or rates directly, via LinkedIn or e-mail.
