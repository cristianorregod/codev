## My two favorite projects

The work I'm proudest of is the Vendor sourcing recommendation engine and the IDRA MCP Server. Both started as my own initiatives, and both turn access to data into useful decisions for different processes.

## Vendor sourcing recommendation engine

It tackles the sales team's main bottleneck, the task they spend the most time on: finding vendors for the items they're asked for. The project came from studying an existing quoting bot and proposing to improve it with several search levels and multiple sources. I was its only developer for 2 months, from data extraction to the API service.

The engine searches the internal history, the vendor catalog through RAG and the internet, and suggests 3 scored vendors for each item. As a result, users' operational time drops by about 70%, depending on the number of items. In an offline evaluation, item search reached a hit@1 of 99.8% with brand and part number.

## IDRA MCP Server

The IDRA MCP Server does it at a more global level: it brings access to the company's data to many processes and users. Each person builds their own metrics by chatting with an assistant like Claude or ChatGPT instead of opening a ticket: a metric that could take up to a week is now available in minutes, and every access stays traceable to a specific user.

I designed the architecture and built the foundation on my own; then I trained the BI team to create new tools following that architecture. It's a project that keeps evolving.

## What I'm passionate about

What I'm most passionate about is getting technology to solve everyday problems. These two projects reflect that well: they're not technology for its own sake, but time people get back to focus on their work.
