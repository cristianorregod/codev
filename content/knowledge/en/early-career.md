## Before Colraices

Before 2022, when I joined Colraices, I worked at Colegio Técnico Comercial Diocesano Santa María, a school where I had two roles: Web Master and computer science teacher.

## Web Master

As Web Master I built the school's website and its inventory system.

## Computer science teacher

As a computer science teacher I mainly taught programming to students in grades 8 to 11. That teaching experience is still with me today in the mentoring I do at IDICO.

## The move to Colraices

In January 2022 I joined Colraices as a Full Stack Developer. There I co-founded and developed the backend and frontend of the credit viability and credit line system with partner bank rules, which is in production today, and I led the development of the automated credit analysis, which cut the evaluation from 2 hours to 5 minutes per client and enabled self-service.
