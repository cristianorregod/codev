## Teaching is part of how I work

I like helping other people grow, and today I do it in several ways at IDICO (Industrial Distributors International Co.): through focused mentoring, by guiding a teammate who wants to move into development, and by training other teams to build on the solutions I design.

## SQL mentoring for the analytics team

At IDICO I've given basic SQL mentoring to the company's analytics team. We covered basic syntax, the structure of a query, joins and subqueries, among other topics.

## Mentoring a teammate who wants to move into development

I'm also mentoring a teammate from the support area who wants to move into software development. I guide his learning with study guides and, as he progresses, I assign him real company challenges of increasing difficulty.

The goal is for him to join the applications team in January, working on backend, frontend, AI, SQL and NetSuite.

## Training the BI team on the IDRA MCP Server

On the IDRA MCP Server I built the foundation on my own during the first months. Then the BI team joined and I taught them to create new tools following the project's architecture. Since then that team builds analytics tools and I handle code review and deployments.

## Previous teaching experience

Before focusing on software development, I was a computer science teacher at Colegio Técnico Comercial Diocesano Santa María, where I mainly taught programming to students in grades 8 to 11.
