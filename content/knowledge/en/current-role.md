## My role at IDICO

I'm Applications Lead at IDICO (Industrial Distributors International Co.), an industrial distribution company focused on procurement and supply chain. I joined in May 2024 as a Software Engineer and today I lead a team of 3 developers. My work combines three things: defining the solutions architecture, leading the team that builds it, and proposing new software and AI initiatives.

## Technical decisions and planning

I make the team's technical decisions: the architecture, the technologies we use and the work plans. From there I assign each developer's milestones, coordinate the team and manage project execution until it reaches production. I also set the development and deployment standards: conventions, testing and CI/CD.

## Proposing software and AI initiatives

An important part of my role is spotting operational processes that can be automated and proposing how to do it. Some examples:

- The Vendor sourcing recommendation engine, which I proposed starting from an existing quoting bot and which cut the sales team's operational time by about 70%.
- The RFQ AI vendor response automation, my own initiative, which removed manual transcription and cut the user’s operational time by 45%.
- The IDRA MCP Server, which lets each user build their own metrics in minutes.

## Integrating AI with the company's systems

I integrate LLM models with corporate platforms (NetSuite, Salesforce and Microsoft 365) via APIs, with authentication, auditing and traceability for every AI action. On the Walle logistics AI agent, for example, as lead developer I built the layer that turns the agent's suggestions into real actions: emails to vendors, Teams notifications and ERP updates.

## Staying up to date

I keep track of new technology trends to identify which ones we can bring into our workflows and which ones help optimize the company's processes.

## I also advise Colraices

In parallel, since June 2024 I've been a Software Consultant and Technical Advisor at Colraices, a financial and real estate services company: I advise on software and AI architecture, coordinate the development team and look after the operational continuity and security of the credit systems.
