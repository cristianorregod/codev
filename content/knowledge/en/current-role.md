## Leading the team

I'm the Applications Lead at IDICO (Industrial Distributors International Co.). I lead a team of 3 developers.

## What I do day to day

I propose software and AI initiatives to meet business needs, mainly automating operational processes.

I make the team's technical decisions: the architecture, the technologies we use and the work plans. I also assign milestones to each developer, coordinate the team and manage project execution.

## Staying up to date

I keep up with new technology trends to identify which ones we can incorporate into our workflows and which ones help optimize the company's processes.
