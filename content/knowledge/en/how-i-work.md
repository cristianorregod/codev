## How I approach problems

I'm detail-oriented and analytical. I don't like to rush: before making any assumption, I take the time to analyze the problem properly.

## Methodology

I don't stick to a single methodology. I adapt and choose the one that best solves the problem in the best possible time.

## Looking for the simplest solution

I always look for the simplest solution. Not everything needs a big or complex development.

## Working with business areas

With business areas I work in a user-oriented way: first I understand their process, then I see what can be improved. Sometimes the user is overcomplicating something that can be simpler, and sometimes they already have the solution in their hands. Not everything needs development, but it's important to understand each process, the business and the operation.
