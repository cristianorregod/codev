## The kind of projects I take on

For independent projects I don't limit myself to one type of work: I can build anything from a landing page to a custom system, or a personal or business AI agent. I'm open to any possibility.

## What I ask from clients: a full discovery process

My only condition is that the client commits to the full discovery process. I don't just want to build a pretty website: I want to understand the whole background to add as much value as possible, and that sometimes takes time to talk things through.

## How it connects with my services

On the services page I describe my three service lines (corporate websites and landing pages, e-commerce and custom software) and my process, which always starts with discovery: understanding the business, the goal and the real constraints. I integrate AI when it adds real value, not because it's trendy.

## Rates

I prefer to discuss rates and quotes directly, via LinkedIn or e-mail, based on the scope of each project.
