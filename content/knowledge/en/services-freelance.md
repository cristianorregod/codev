## The kind of projects I take on

In independent projects I don't limit myself to one type of work: I can build anything from a landing page to a custom system, or an AI agent for personal use or for a business. I'm open to any possibility.

## My three service lines

- Corporate website and landing pages: for professionals, personal brands and service companies that want their website to build authority and generate opportunities.
- E-commerce: for brands selling physical or digital products that need a reliable sales channel, with a good checkout, payments and a clear catalog.
- Custom software: for companies with manual processes or fragmented systems, with internal dashboards, workflows and integrations with APIs, ERPs or existing tools.

## What I ask from the client: a full discovery process

My only condition is that the client commits to the whole discovery process. I don't just want to build a pretty website: I want to understand the full background so I can add as much value as possible, and sometimes that takes time talking things through.

## My work process

I work in five stages: discovery (understanding the business, the goal and the real constraints), scope (deliverables, priorities and a staged plan), design (validating the experience, the messaging and the main flow), iterative development focused on quality and performance, and optimization (measuring, adjusting and evolving based on results).

## AI when it adds real value

I integrate AI when it adds real value, not because it's trendy. It makes sense when there are repetitive processes with enough volume, minimum reliable information and a clear, measurable business goal. It doesn't make sense when it's wanted as decoration, when there's no defined process, or when the digital foundation still has structural problems.

## The experience behind it

What I offer as a freelancer is backed by what I build every day: custom software and AI applied to real processes. For example, at Colraices I designed and built the Colraices Multi-Agent AI Assistant, which doubled the sales advisors’ client management capacity.
