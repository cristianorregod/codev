## Where I am now

Today I'm Applications Lead at IDICO (Industrial Distributors International Co.), an industrial distribution company where I started as a Software Engineer in May 2024. I lead a team of 3 developers and I make the technical decisions: architecture, technologies, work plans, each developer's milestones and coordinating execution. I also advise on software and AI architecture and coordinate the development team at Colraices.

## The roles I'm looking for

I'm looking for roles where architecture, technical leadership and AI are at the center of the work. Specifically:

- Solutions Architect.
- Tech Lead.
- AI Engineer / Software AI Engineer.

These are the three areas I already work in today at IDICO, and that's where I want to keep growing.

## What I'd bring as a Solutions Architect

At IDICO I define the company's AI and integration solutions architecture: I connect LLM models with NetSuite, Salesforce and Microsoft 365 via APIs, with authentication, auditing and traceability for every AI action. A good example is the IDRA MCP Server: I designed its layered architecture and documented it along with the conventions for adding tools, which let another team build on top of it without breaking the design. I've also worked on end-to-end integrations such as the Salesforce ⇄ NetSuite integration platform, which replaced manual changes in both systems with real-time bidirectional sync.

## What I'd bring as a Tech Lead

I already do this job: I lead 3 developers, define the architecture and technologies, put together the work plans, assign each person's milestones and coordinate project execution. I also set the team's development and deployment standards: conventions, testing and CI/CD. On the Sales order approval AI agent I started as a developer and then took over coordinating testing and the following phases, and on the IDRA MCP Server I handle code review and deployments for the tools the BI team now builds.

## What I'd bring as an AI Engineer

I bring AI into real operational processes and I measure the outcome. I proposed several of these initiatives myself, such as the sourcing engine and the IDRA MCP Server:

- Vendor sourcing recommendation engine: cuts the operational time the sales team spends finding vendors by about 70%.
- Sales order approval AI agent: validating an order went from 30 minutes to 1 hour down to seconds, and approved orders doubled.
- IDRA MCP Server: a metric that could take up to a week as a ticket is now available in minutes.

I work with agents, RAG, MCP servers and LLMs with structured outputs, and when the process calls for it I leave the final decision in a person's hands.

## Close to users and to the team

Before building, I work closely with users to understand their process. The sourcing engine, for example, came from studying how an existing quoting bot worked before proposing how to improve it. And I care about the team growing: I've given SQL mentoring to the analytics team, I'm mentoring a support teammate who is moving into development, and I taught the BI team to build tools on top of the IDRA MCP Server architecture.

## What I look for in a company

I'm looking for a place where I can propose ideas and initiatives, have visibility over the whole process and not just one piece of it, own the technical definitions and the architecture, and work with AI applied to real business problems.

- I deliver measurable results, not just prototypes: the Vendor sourcing recommendation engine cut operational time by about 70%, the Sales order approval AI agent doubled approved orders, the IDRA MCP Server delivers metrics in minutes instead of up to a week and, at Colraices, the multi-agent assistant turned an analysis of about 3 hours per client into under 10 minutes.
